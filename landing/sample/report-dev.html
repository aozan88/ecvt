Expected: Each identical submission is not applied more than once by the wallet's own rules; the source is debited at most once per intended transfer, and the sum of balances is conserved.

CheckExpectedObservedDetail
wallet.replay_applied_onceone debit for a replayed transfer3 distinct source balancesthe same transfer, replayed 3 times, moved money 3 times (source balance after each: [80.0, 60.0, 40.0])

Judge (fail): Three identical transfers of 20.0 were each accepted with 200 and each changed state: the source fell from 100.0 to 40.0 and the destination rose from 0.0 to 60.0, so the transfer was applied three times rather than once.
